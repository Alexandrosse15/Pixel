In Elementis TD, the beginner's biggest mistake is simple: placing your most powerful towers everywhere and watching, helpless, as your enemies become invincible. The whole game revolves around a single gauge, Corruption, and knowing how to tame it makes the difference between a freed kingdom and a humiliating defeat. This guide gives you the reflexes to win without feeding the beast.

![Elementis TD, the balance between corrupting and pure towers](/images/elementistd/screenshot-4.webp)

## How does Corruption work?

This is the heart of the game, so you might as well understand it perfectly. Your most devastating towers, Fire, Lightning, Granite, Frost and Sniper, are called corrupting: they hit hardest, but every time they deal damage, they raise the Corruption gauge. And the higher Corruption climbs, the more your enemies' health and speed increase. In other words, your best weapons literally make your opponents tougher.

Conversely, pure towers cleanse Corruption. They deal less raw damage, but they bring the gauge back down and prevent the vicious spiral where enemies become unplayable. All of Elementis TD's strategy therefore comes down to juggling power and purification: hitting hard when needed, purifying before the gauge reaches a critical level. Never letting Corruption run away is the first rule of survival.

## Which towers should you favour and when?

The beginner's temptation is to bet everything on corrupting towers, because they reassure with their big numbers. That is a trap. The right approach is to build a base of pure towers to keep Corruption under control, then add corrupting towers as an occasional accelerator, to activate on hard waves or resistant enemies.

Concretely, think in terms of rhythm. Early in a wave, when the pressure is low, let your pure towers work to keep the gauge low and even bring it down. When a dense wave or a tough enemy arrives, that is the moment to unleash the power of corrupting towers to bring it down fast, accepting a temporary rise in Corruption. Then, as soon as the threat is cleared, return to purification. This sawtooth management, alternating power surges and cleansing phases, is the key to a controlled game.

## How do you use element fusion?

Element fusion is what gives Elementis TD its depth and replayability. Combining elements lets you create towers with new effects, and that is where the advanced strategies hide. Do not settle for placing basic towers: experiment with fusions to find the combinations that maximise damage while limiting Corruption generation, or that boost the effectiveness of your pure towers.

The ideal is to build a build identity around two or three elements that complement each other, rather than scattering your resources across the whole board. A coherent base, where each tower reinforces the logic of the others, will always be more effective than a mismatched assortment. Test, note what works against which enemy types, and refine your composition kingdom after kingdom. Mastering fusions is what separates the player who suffers Corruption from the one who pilots it.

## How do you survive the Eternal Vigil?

Once the seven kingdoms are freed, the Eternal Vigil is the real test: an endless roguelite mode where difficulty rises indefinitely. Here, managing Corruption becomes even more critical, because over a long game, the slightest negligence is paid for by a fatal enemy runaway. Discipline is your best ally: never give in to the ease of corrupting towers without a purification plan behind.

The key to longevity in the Eternal Vigil is anticipation. Always plan your purification capacity according to the power you deploy, keep a safety margin on the gauge, and adapt your build to the rising power of the waves rather than suffering it. The best scores come from patient, rigorous management, not from poorly controlled power flourishes. In the Eternal Vigil, it is consistency that wins, never haste.

## Elementis TD FAQ

**What are the corrupting towers?**

Fire, Lightning, Granite, Frost and Sniper. They deal the most damage but raise Corruption with each hit, boosting the enemies' health and speed.

**What are pure towers for?**

They cleanse Corruption and bring it back down. Less powerful in raw damage, they are indispensable to prevent your enemies from becoming unplayable.

**How do you avoid being overwhelmed by Corruption?**

Build a base of pure towers to keep the gauge low, and use corrupting towers only in occasional bursts on hard waves, before re-purifying immediately.

**How does element fusion work?**

Combining elements creates towers with new effects. Build a coherent build around two or three complementary elements rather than scattering your resources.

**How do you progress in the Eternal Vigil?**

Through discipline and anticipation: always keep a purification margin proportional to the power deployed, and favour patient management over risky flourishes.
