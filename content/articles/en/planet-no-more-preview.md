Planet No More has a simple ambition: making you destroy planets, right down to the core. Your fleet fires, the 3D planet cracks and erodes in real time, and precious materials scatter into the void. You gather them with your cursor, using upgradable magnets, before you run out of fuel.

![A planet bombarded by a fleet, its fragments scattered](/images/planetnomore/screenshot-1.webp)

It is an arcade incremental with a familiar loop: play a short run, invest the haul in an upgrade tree (firepower, fuel, collection efficiency), and head back out stronger. Reaching the core earns a Celestial Core, the currency that unlocks the next target. Comets sometimes pass by and grant a temporary boost if you catch them. The studio advises thinking, before each purchase, about what ended your last run.

![The shop run by a captain cat](/images/planetnomore/screenshot-4.webp)

WiseMonkeES' game, published by Rogue Duck Interactive and Gamersky, has gained new planets and new ships through its demo updates, including a Super Bazooka. There is also a captain cat running the shop. Out on 4 December, demo available.
