Convenience store sims have become a genre of their own, and someone was bound to subvert it. Shark Mart does so with an irresistible pitch: you and your friends are sharks disguised as dolphins, and your underwater shop is a front.

![Two disguised sharks in front of The Fishfather shop](/images/sharkmart/screenshot-1.webp)

By day, you do honest work. You stock the shelves with sponges and water bottles, serve the locals, keep the place spotless. You also win the trust of the whales by polishing their bellies and fins until they shine. Whale loyalty, the Steam page explains, is your ticket in.

Then you kidnap your own customers and cook them in the hidden basement under the shop.

![A shark chef at the stove, in the secret kitchen](/images/sharkmart/screenshot-4.webp)

Naturally, the fish police investigate. You have to clean the crime scene, lie through your teeth in interrogations, and never show your fangs. The game plays in co-op, and you can already picture the best moments: one friend panicking in the interrogation, another who forgot to wipe the counter.

The demo was pulled from Steam on 19 November so the team could focus on launch, on 26 November. The winner of a Discord contest will see their Halloween item added to the game as an easter egg.

Your best lie to the fish police belongs on the [InsertCoins Discord](https://discord.gg/473FE3dWvw).
