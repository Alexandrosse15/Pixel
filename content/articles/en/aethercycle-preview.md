AetherCycle ticks every box of the classic action RPG, with one big difference: it is all played in first person. You explore an open fantasy world, complete quests, descend into dungeons, caves and ruins, and visit villages, camps, forts and castles.

![A lit gothic cathedral at the top of a staircase](/images/aethercycle/screenshot-2.webp)

Combat mixes melee weapons, ranged attacks and magic. You level up, raise your attributes, unlock active abilities and passive skills, and combine them with gear that has randomised stats and rarity tiers. Crafting fills the gaps in your build. All of it plays solo or in online co-op, with shared progression.

![A flaming sword in first person](/images/aethercycle/screenshot-4.webp)

DEEP INTERACTIVE premiered its launch trailer through IGN. It states on Steam that generative tools may assist early concepts, but all final content is made by the team. The game contains bloody violence. Early Access on 4 December: that is when we will see whether the ambition holds.
