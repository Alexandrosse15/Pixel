Your father disappeared, leaving you a run-down workshop and an enormous debt. You have until a deadline to pay it off. Starship Crafter starts there, which is a more narrative opening than the average sim published by PlayWay (House Flipper, Car Mechanic Simulator).

![The workshop and a holographic projection of a ship under construction](/images/starshipcrafter/screenshot-1.webp)

The loop is that of a mechanic sim, transplanted into space. You start by salvaging broken objects for parts, which you turn into basic components. You unlock more advanced components and blueprints, then build your own ships. Contracts come from rival factions, racing pilots, desperate strangers and important figures, and each one advances your reputation and your repayments.

Money goes back into the workshop: upgraded workstations, underground hangar floors to repair, patch and replace parts. Meanwhile, the galaxy lives on in the news, with elections, races and conflicts unfolding with or without you. You will have to choose which faction to side with, and that choice shapes the ending.

![A ship on its launch pad, in orange light](/images/starshipcrafter/screenshot-2.webp)

Rysesoft states that generative tools were used to produce base images for some 2D assets, later reworked by its artist, along with generated voices for some lines. A free prologue has been on Steam since July, to get a feel before release on 27 November.

Your first working ship deserves a photo on the [InsertCoins Discord](https://discord.gg/473FE3dWvw). Even if it does not take off.
