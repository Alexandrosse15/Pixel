Somewhere among ten million pixels, there is a golden one. The whole game is about finding it.

10 Million Pixels is a minimalist incremental. You launch balls, they bounce and break pixels, and broken pixels serve as currency to launch even more balls. The world is procedurally generated, with handcrafted indestructible objects and upgrade pixels that unlock new balls.

![Thousands of balls digging through a world of coloured pixels](/images/10millionpixels/screenshot-2.webp)

There are seven, each with its own ability. The blue one only moves in straight lines, horizontal or vertical. The red one shatters pixels around its point of impact. The green one poisons them, and the poison slowly eats into neighbouring pixels. The fun comes from combining them, and from the moment the screen fills with thousands of balls bouncing at once. The physics engine is said to hold a stable 60+ frames per second under those conditions.

The demo only offered a world of 50,000 pixels. The final version is two hundred times larger.

![The ball upgrade tree, icon by icon](/images/10millionpixels/screenshot-3.webp)

The developer, BATTLE LAB, says the project was meant to be "a small incremental game without any fluff", and grew a lot over a year: a full redesign, the introduction of the Golden Pixel, and a soundtrack by Pulsewith (Justin Skomarovsky), who also scored The Loopler. The game passed 100,000 wishlists in October.

**Update, 27 November:** the release, planned for 27 November, has been delayed again, to 10 December. The developer explains the game was not yet as polished as intended, and still lacked a few important features and some balancing. An official Discord server has opened in the meantime.

The first person to find the Golden Pixel gets an official announcement on the [InsertCoins Discord](https://discord.gg/473FE3dWvw). We want the screenshot.
