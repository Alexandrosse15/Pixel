Iron Dawn has the qualities we credited it with in [our preview](/articles/iron-dawn-preview). It is an isometric mech shooter in 5 to 15 minute missions, where every contract asks the same question: complete the objectives for the full payout, or extract early with what you have already earned. The low-poly scenery collapses into cubes under fire, and it is very satisfying.

![A mech firing at the edge of a base, in a low-poly landscape](/images/irondawn/screenshot-5.webp)

The most enjoyable part is the shooting. Projectile travel time matters, and you have to learn to lead targets and judge the convergence point of arm-mounted guns. Mech fans have pointed out that few games in the genre bother with that. Your choice of chassis (bipedal, quadrupedal or tracked), turret and weapons genuinely changes how you play.

The problem is progression. The start is very punishing with starting gear, then, once past the first hurdle, the game becomes far too easy. And it is short: several players bought everything and finished the last set of missions in about three hours.

![A mech in a forest, its shots tracing a line to the enemy](/images/irondawn/screenshot-6.webp)

And then there is the unease. In Iron Dawn, everything you destroy pays out. Including houses and hospitals. The game offers no comment and no context: you are a mercenary, and destruction is a revenue stream. Several players picked up on it, one saying it felt like committing a war crime. You could read it as a satire of mercenary work. The game does nothing to make it read as anything other than a mechanic.

![A desert town from above, amid the gunfire](/images/irondawn/screenshot-7.webp)

At 5.21 euros, it is a decent little action game that recalls MechCommander 2 at its best moments. Polylab is responsive (it has already added an option to turn off the depth-of-field blur). But the progression needs rebalancing, and some thought about what you blow up.

Extract early or risk it all? The debate continues on the [InsertCoins Discord](https://discord.gg/473FE3dWvw).
