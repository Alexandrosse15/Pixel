Grandpa left you three things: the farm, a bag of carrot seeds and his old revolver. Farmed & Dangerous' Steam page points out that you are going to need all three.

![A first-person revolver facing a flock of crows over a vegetable patch](/images/farmeddangerous/screenshot-1.webp)

It is a first-person roguelite that alternates day and night. By day, crows arrive in waves, ever more numerous, and you have to shoot them while still watering your crops: every peck and every hour without water is a harvest lost for good. You start with the revolver, then come the shotgun, the submachine gun, the grenade launcher, the rocket launcher and, for unexplained reasons, a katana. Turrets, sprinklers and scarecrows bolster your defences. You can even grab a crow pecking at your tomatoes and hurl it away, or call in a crop-duster water bomber.

At night, the shooting stops. You plant, harvest, shop and prepare for the next day.

![A farm at night, pumpkins lit by the house](/images/farmeddangerous/screenshot-3.webp)

The clever idea is economic: killing earns nothing (except golden birds). Crops are what keep the farm alive, and a delivery truck comes by every few days demanding an ever larger order. If it is not ready, the run ends. Up to three farmers can play together, with a single till, proximity voice chat, and a flock that grows with the team.

Limina Games' title has passed 10,000 wishlists and comes out on 4 December.

The best crow shooters are expected on the [InsertCoins Discord](https://discord.gg/473FE3dWvw). The katana remains a mystery.
