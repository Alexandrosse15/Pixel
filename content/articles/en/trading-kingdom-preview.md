Bandits have ravaged your village. Everything has to start again: lead the village, farm the fields, and build a fortune buying and selling at the markets. Trading Kingdom's goal is not to conquer land by war, but to become the most powerful merchant in northern Italy.

![A rider with a shield on a country path](/images/tradingkingdom/screenshot-1.webp)

The world is open, centred on Venice. You travel through villages, castles and towns, step inside, and hunt for good deals. The world map shows your territories, those of the three rival merchants and the land no one owns yet. You have to win the right to run castle and town markets, take control of new regions and outpace your competitors. Hunting, fishing and farming round out your income, and the money goes back into the village.

![The regional map, with villages and trade routes](/images/tradingkingdom/screenshot-2.webp)

The project is developed solo by 99 Interactive, whose demo dates back to the October 2024 Steam Next Fest. The developer then took time to rework trading, combat, dialogue and towns. The Steam page discloses generative tools for some interface elements, textures, localisation and part of the code. Early Access on 4 December.

Budding merchants have a trading post on the [InsertCoins Discord](https://discord.gg/473FE3dWvw). Good deals change hands there.
