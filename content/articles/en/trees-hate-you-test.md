The pitch fits in one line: you have finished your picnic, you need to get home, and the forest will not let you.

Trees Hate You is a trap game, in the tradition of platformers designed to make you rage (Cat Mario, Level Devil), but in 3D and in the woods. Every tree is suspect. The one that looks like it is casting shade is going to fall on you. The obvious path is trapped. So is the hidden one. And the game turns your expectations against you with admirable consistency: as soon as you understand a trap, the next one plays on the fact that you understood it.

![A small hiker on a stone path, between innocent fir trees](/images/treeshateyou/screenshot-1.webp)

What saves it from pure cruelty is the gag. Every death is staged like a sketch. The demo added a detail I love: if your character wears glasses, they fly off when you die. So you die a lot, but you laugh almost as much.

The game crosses several biomes, and that is where you realise the developer, Tykenn, has plenty in reserve. The Christmas forest, with its candy canes and decorated tree, is particularly vicious. The desert has its cacti, and yes, the palm trees hate you too.

![A snowy Christmas forest, candy canes and snowman included](/images/treeshateyou/screenshot-2.webp)

A death counter and a timer, added after the demo, turn every attempt into a small personal challenge. You collect hats along the way, and eventually find an axe (what it is for is a surprise I will not spoil).

![A tumbleweed rolling across the desert toward the player](/images/treeshateyou/screenshot-4.webp)

It is short, it is cruel, and at 6.77 euros, it is exactly what it promises. 95% positive reviews on launch day and more than 600 players online: the joke lands.
