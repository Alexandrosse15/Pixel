The title is a programme, and it does not lie. When Chests Rain Tons of Loot is an incremental where you drop your heroes anywhere on the field. They fight slimes on their own, and you collect the loot that falls. Everything lands in a treasure chest.

![A rain of pink slimes over a field, with heroes in the middle](/images/whenchestsrain/screenshot-1.webp)

The chest is the heart of the loop. Filled past certain thresholds, it upgrades to Silver, then Gold, then Diamond, and pays out more. And when time runs out, it explodes and rains its whole contents down for one last grab. It is exactly the kind of cheap dopamine you own up to.

Between runs, a huge skill tree: more heroes, harder landings, more arrows, more gold. Archers, thunder mages, a Barbarian who rallies the troops and tosses slimes into piles. There is also a forge where you upgrade gear at a risk, collectible cards, a new map and a boss per land, and surprises: golden slimes, treasure goblin, mimic.

![The skill tree, node by node](/images/whenchestsrain/screenshot-2.webp)

It is a small game, from LeenGameDev, riding the wave of loot-shower incrementals. The demo is available to check whether the loop hooks you before 25 November.

Your biggest Diamond chest deserves a screenshot on the [InsertCoins Discord](https://discord.gg/473FE3dWvw), right before it blows.
