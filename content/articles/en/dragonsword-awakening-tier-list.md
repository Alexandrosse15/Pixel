With nineteen playable heroes and a combat system entirely built on tag-team play and status ailments, DragonSword Awakening doesn't make choosing your team obvious. Not all characters are equal, and above all, their power depends as much on their role in the combo chain as on their raw stats. This tier list ranks the best heroes by tier, explains why they stand out, and details the team compositions that chain combos without letting the enemy breathe. To master the combat system itself, poise breaking, Signal Skills and invincibility frames, pair this with our [complete DragonSword Awakening guide](/guides/dragonsword-awakening-guide).

## How to read this tier list?

A warning first, because it's essential. The game just launched, and a launch tier list reflects the current meta, not a truth carved in stone. Rankings will evolve with updates, rebalances and the community deepening the combos. So take this ranking as a solid base for investing your resources without going wrong, not as dogma.

Above all, in DragonSword Awakening, a hero isn't valued on their own but by their place in the status-ailment chain. A good team covers three roles: setup (applying an ailment like Freeze, Poison or Shock), breaking (breaking poise and launching into the air via Knockdown or Airborne), and burst (exploding the damage during the juggle, typically with Bleed). The best characters are those who excellently fill one of these roles and slot into the best synergies. It's that lens, not stats alone, that structures the tier list below.

## Who are the best characters (Tier 1)?

Here are the safe bets, the heroes to prioritize to build a formidable team from the start.

- **Castella (Windrunner Lance)**: the roster's best Airborne applier, and an almost mandatory centerpiece. She launches enemies into the air with rare reliability and relaunches already-knocked-down targets, extending the aerial chains that make combat so satisfying.
- **Roxy (Crimson Striker)**: the Bleed specialist, who stacks the ailment and shines in burst during the Airborne state. She's your explosive damage source once the target is airborne.
- **Renia (Frost Alchemist)**: the best Freeze setter, via her Spirit Ice Mist. She opens the freeze combos and sets up the pressure others convert into damage.
- **Alex (Frontline Breaker)**: the king of poise breaking, with his huge greatsword poise damage and his Knockdown application. Indispensable for opening enemy guards.

These four heroes form the backbone of the current meta, because together they cover the three key roles of the combo chain, setup, breaking and burst.

## Which characters are solid (Tier 2)?

Just below, these heroes are excellent picks, often complementary to the Tier 1s, and perfectly viable in a well-thought-out team.

- **Ornette (Magitech Frost Gunner)**: a Freeze builder who also extends aerial chains, an excellent alternative or complement to Renia.
- **Lute (Fated Blade, protagonist)**: the team's Swiss army knife, at once support, damage source and healer, able to trigger Freeze. His versatility makes him a precious pillar once your main carries are established.
- **Theresia (Holy Sanctum Inquisitor)**: the Shock specialist, who stacks the ailment and severely punishes downed (Knockdown) targets.
- **Aria (Sanctum Channeler)**: a Shock applier who also triggers Knockdown, very useful for chaining breaks.

Don't dismiss these heroes just because they're not Tier 1: their value explodes in the right synergies, and a team smartly built around them outperforms a poorly coordinated Tier 1 trio.

## What are the best teams?

Beyond characters taken individually, it's the compositions that win fights. Two teams stand out particularly in the launch meta, each built around a coherent combo loop.

The first is the **Freeze team**: Renia, Lute and Castella. The logic is crystal clear, Renia sets up Freeze, Lute triggers the icebreaker, and Castella extends with the aerial extension. It's a fluid, versatile composition, carried by Lute's support and healing that make it resilient. The second is the **Stagger and juggle team**: Castella, Alex and Roxy. Here, Alex breaks poise with Knockdown, Castella launches into the air, and Roxy explodes the damage with Bleed during the juggle. It's a formidable burst machine, chaining break, launch and explosion with no downtime.

In both cases, the lesson is the same: build thinking about the setup, break, burst chain rather than stacking damage dealers. A team balanced across these three roles chains combos that never let the opponent regain control.

## How to level your characters first?

Once your trio is chosen, focus your resources on it rather than spreading them across the whole roster. Invest first in your core carries, and save Lute for a bit later: his support and healing shine mostly once your main characters are established. Since Master Skills unlock at level 30 per hero, it's better to bring three characters to that tier than to sprinkle ten.

On gear, remember the 3+2 set rule: set bonuses are obtained at three pieces, not five, which allows mixed builds like three venomous pieces plus two stun ones. Favor crit damage on chest pieces, which offer the highest main stats, and when reforging, stack the sub-stats matching your hero's element. **Who are the best characters in DragonSword Awakening?**

At launch, Tier 1 gathers Castella (Airborne), Roxy (Bleed and burst), Renia (Freeze) and Alex (Knockdown and poise breaking), because they cover the three key combo roles.

**What is the best team?**

Two compositions dominate: the Freeze team (Renia, Lute, Castella) and the Stagger and juggle team (Castella, Alex, Roxy), each built around a coherent combo loop.

Yes, but not as an absolute priority. His versatility, support, damage and healing, shines mostly once your main carries are established, so develop your core trio first.

**How many characters should you invest in?**
