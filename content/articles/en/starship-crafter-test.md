Starship Crafter belongs to the big family of sims published by PlayWay, the one that includes House Flipper and Car Mechanic Simulator. Everything that defines the genre is here: simple, repeated, satisfying tasks, and a workshop that grows as you earn money. The difference, as we noted in [our preview](/articles/starship-crafter-preview), is the story.

![A ship under repair in the hangar, under neon lights](/images/starshipcrafter/screenshot-5.webp)

Your father disappeared, leaving a run-down workshop and an enormous debt, with a deadline. You start by salvaging broken objects for parts, which you turn into components at workstations. Then you unlock blueprints, and build and repair ships for rival factions, racing pilots and desperate strangers. Each contract pays down a little of the debt and builds your reputation.

The workshop mini-games are the core of the loop: welding a hull plate, rewiring a power unit, patching a breach. They are readable and pleasant, even if they repeat quickly once you have seen them all. The workshop expands through underground hangar floors, and watching it come back to life is the real reward.

![A workstation with its coils and cables](/images/starshipcrafter/screenshot-6.webp)

The galaxy lives in the background through the news (elections, races, conflicts), and you eventually have to pick a faction, which steers the ending. It is a welcome narrative layer in a genre that lacks one, though it stays fairly light.

Launch has its little bugs, and Rysesoft released a first hotfix on day one (a droid stuck in a table, a robot duplicated by a too-quick double repair). The Steam page also discloses that some 2D art starts from generated images reworked by an artist, and that some lines are voiced by generated voices.

![A kiosk in front of a propaganda poster](/images/starshipcrafter/screenshot-7.webp)

At 12.74 euros with the launch discount, it is a good sim for fans of this kind of loop, wrapped in well-done science fiction.

Your first fully restored ship deserves a photo on the [InsertCoins Discord](https://discord.gg/473FE3dWvw). Before delivery, of course.
