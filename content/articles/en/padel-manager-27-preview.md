Padel is the sport that took over tennis clubs in just a few years, and it did not have its own management game yet. Padel Manager 27, from a solo Spanish developer (Sácame de Pobre Games), wants to fill that gap, and does so with an enthusiast's seriousness.

You start with a rented warehouse, two courts and four students. Everything else is yours to build: recruiting, pairing players, planning training, preparing tactics. Then climbing from a tenth-division amateur league to the first, and entering opens and international circuits.

![An exhibition match under the lights, blue court and glass walls](/images/padelmanager27/screenshot-1.webp)

## Every point is really played

That is the central pitch, and it is convincing in the demo. Matches are not a calculated score: every rally is simulated shot by shot, with ball physics and glass walls, and nineteen different shots (lobs, bandejas, víboras, smashes, drop shots, chiquitas). The developer spent weeks on movement, built from hundreds of motion takes, so players stop sliding across the floor. It shows.

The management side is built around people: every player has a personality and ambitions, and a pair needs chemistry as much as skill. You talk to your players, make them promises, and live with the consequences.

![A rally seen from net height, racket in the foreground](/images/padelmanager27/screenshot-3.webp)

You can also play yourself, which is rare enough in the genre to point out. Online matches for two to four players, online and local co-op, up to four on one screen. The management career remains single-player.

The Steam page discloses that some sound effects (ambience, crowd, impacts) and some store images are generated, and that coding assistants were used during development.

![An academy's turquoise court, wide view](/images/padelmanager27/screenshot-5.webp)

Early Access was announced for 16 November. The Steam page now just says "Q4 2026", and the developer expects about a year of Early Access. In the meantime, the demo is free, and it is the best way to see whether the simulation grabs you.

Padel players on the [InsertCoins Discord](https://discord.gg/473FE3dWvw) are invited to judge whether the game's víboras look like theirs.
