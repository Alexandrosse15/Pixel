Dungeonbound was delayed, and its developer apologised on launch day, in a message ending with "I'm sorry for delaying the release and disappointing some of you". In the meantime, the game went from 55,000 to 85,000 wishlists in less than a week. A few hours after launch, more than 3,000 players were online and 93% of reviews were positive.

For a game made by one person, louidev, that is an excellent start. And it is deserved.

![The Paladin, their stat sheet and equipment](/images/dungeonbound/screenshot-4.webp)

## The backpack is the game

Dungeonbound is a first-person dungeon crawler, with a PS1-era CRT filter, where fights play out on their own. Your job is preparation. Each item is a shaped piece to fit onto a grid, Backpack Battles style, and what it touches changes what it does. Each attack travels around the board and triggers the items it crosses: placement matters as much as the item.

And there are more than 540 of them. Weapons that scale with your missing HP, others with your gold. Cursed pieces that bring a fortune with a real downside. Duplicates merge up to level 10, and the forge combines any two items into a hybrid that inherits from both. It is the kind of system where you spend twenty minutes moving a potion by one square, and those twenty minutes are the best of the evening.

![The wheel of fortune in the middle of the dungeon](/images/dungeonbound/screenshot-2.webp)

## Three ways down

The Long Haul is the incremental mode: seven heroes, each with permanent progression, and a death that costs floors, not hours. That is where to start.

The Ascension is the roguelike mode, with 32 heroes to unlock, five hearts and a relic to choose on each of the first five floors. Its best idea: the rivals you meet are real builds from other players, item by item. If one beats you, you can watch the replay and steal their trick. And your own board will go on to haunt someone else's run.

Co-op, finally, lets you run the Ascension with four people, five shared hearts, and items passed around by dropping them onto a teammate's board. The first patch, released the same day, fixed teams being split onto different paths. There are bugs left to hunt, which is normal.

![A forgotten chest glowing in the darkness of a corridor](/images/dungeonbound/screenshot-5.webp)

The interface is dense, almost intimidating at first, and reading the automatic fights takes some getting used to. But at 10.34 euros, it is one of the richest games of the autumn.
