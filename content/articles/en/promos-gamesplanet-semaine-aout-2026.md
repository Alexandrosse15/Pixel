## Three pieces of advice before reaching for the card

**Be wary of Deluxe editions.** On many of the lines above, the Deluxe costs twice the base version for a pack of skins. The exceptions are real (Borderlands 3 Ultimate, Civilization VI Anthology, RE Village Gold) because they contain actual expansions. Look at what is inside, not at the percentage.

**A big percentage on an old game is not a bargain, it is depreciation.** A 2013 title at -90 per cent is simply worth its age. The right question is not how far it has fallen, but whether you will play it.

**And the prices here are the French store's, in euros.** The links take you automatically to your regional store, where the amounts differ.

Every sale above ends on **4 September at 2am**, except the Capcom sale which closes **tomorrow**. If you only click one link in this article, make it that one.
