Siberian Prison Builder borrows Prison Architect's structure and moves it to the taiga, where winter never ends. You run an isolated labour camp: build it, hire the staff, feed the prisoners and, above all, keep them warm.

![A camp canteen, prisoners at the tables, snow all around](/images/siberianprisonbuilder/screenshot-2.webp)

Cold is the first enemy. Stoves need wood, blizzards cut the road and bring down the power line, killing the searchlights. A stove left unattended too long can set a whole cell block ablaze. The prisoners smuggle in shivs, steal tools and form gangs, and a fight can turn into a riot. The game hides nothing of the place's brutality: guards search, restrain, beat or execute, and the camp lives with the consequences.

You draw buildings at whatever size you want, and their layout is generated automatically. Six prisoner categories, from minimum security to death row, and fifteen building types.

![A prisoner trying to climb the barbed wire under a searchlight](/images/siberianprisonbuilder/screenshot-3.webp)

The other half of the game is Escape mode: you play a prisoner, steal a weapon, recruit accomplices and try to get over the wire before the watchtowers spot you. Up to eight players can share a camp online, some as wardens, others as escapees, switching sides whenever they like.

Rogue History has not set a release date yet, but a demo is available and the game is already receiving updates, including Steam leaderboards.
