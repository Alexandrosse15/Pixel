Some concepts need only a single sentence to win you over. Cat Squeeze is a cat that slips through pipes and blows up walls to solve puzzles. You could stop there, tick the cute box and move on. Except under its feline comfort-blanket looks, this little puzzle hides a far craftier mechanic than it lets on, and it's precisely that marriage of charm and intelligence that makes it so endearing. You come for the cat, you stay to rack your brain.

![Cat Squeeze, the cozy puzzle where the cat Coco slips through and blows up obstacles](/images/catsqueeze/screenshot-1.webp)

## The context

Cat Squeeze is a cozy, brain-teasing puzzle co-developed by Cattery Studio and Pone Games, available on PC since 1 July 2026. You guide Coco, a cat who must carve a path through mazes by pushing boxes, slipping through pipe networks and triggering well-placed explosions. The game belongs to the Sokoban lineage, that kind of puzzle where you push blocks to clear a passage, but it adds two of its own mechanics that renew the formula. A flawless launch reception, with 100% positive reviews, sign of a little game that found its recipe.

## The gameplay: feline Sokoban, explosions and pipes

The heart of Cat Squeeze rests on the tried-and-true Sokoban base: push boxes to clear the path and reach the objective. It's a familiar, legible and satisfying foundation, where every move counts and a badly pushed box can doom a level. But the game doesn't settle for that, and that's where its personality shines. Coco can trigger controlled explosions that destroy walls and obstacles, opening new paths, provided you aim the right detonation point. That mechanic turns the classic puzzle into a planning exercise where you no longer just skirt obstacles, you blow them up in the right spot.

The second find is the ability to slip through pipes. You slide Coco into a network of pipes to reposition her, find new paths or reach otherwise inaccessible areas. That mechanic adds a clever spatial dimension, where you have to think in three dimensions and anticipate which pipe to come out of. The combination of the three pillars, push, explode, slip through, produces puzzles that scale in complexity with elegance, without ever losing their legibility. You feel the care poured into the level design, each one a little theorem to solve, and it's that rigor under the gentleness that makes all the game's appeal.

![Cat Squeeze, the targeted explosions and pipe network that renew Sokoban](/images/catsqueeze/screenshot-2.webp)

## What sticks

You have to stay honest about the nature of the proposition. Cat Squeeze is a little game, in the noble sense, but a little game all the same: a cozy puzzle to consume in sessions rather than a long-haul adventure. The Sokoban genre, even enriched, stays a niche pleasure, reserved for whoever loves racking their brain over placement and logic puzzles. Whoever seeks action, story or grand spectacle will walk on, because that's simply not the title's ambition. It's an owned brain-teaser, to approach for what it is.

The other reservation is lifespan, inherent to the genre. A puzzle of this type lives on the quantity and inventiveness of its levels, and everything will depend on its generosity to last over time. The risk, with a puzzle, is exhausting it once the mechanics are mastered and the levels solved, with little replayability beyond. But that's the genre's fate, and Cat Squeeze doesn't claim otherwise: it offers a lovely, charming cerebral parenthesis, to savor at your own pace, without presenting itself as a marathon game. Taken as such, it fulfills its purpose with genuine know-how.

![Cat Squeeze, levels that scale in complexity with elegance and legibility](/images/catsqueeze/screenshot-3.webp)

## What we take away

Cat Squeeze is a success that proves a cute concept can hide a genuinely clever brain-teaser. Its Sokoban base, enriched by the targeted explosions and the pipe mechanic, produces placement and planning puzzles as satisfying as they are elegantly designed, where Coco's feline charm is never a cover for emptiness but the delightful dressing of a game with ideas. The flawless launch reception isn't lying: it's a polished, clever, endearing little puzzle, cut out for whoever loves racking their brain in good spirits.

You just have to approach it for what it is: a cozy puzzle to savor in small touches, not an epic adventure. The Sokoban genre stays niche, its lifespan will depend on the generosity of its levels, and it won't speak to whoever seeks action or story. But for the puzzle fan, or simply for whoever wants a charming cerebral break between two big games, Cat Squeeze is a feline gem not to let slip. You come for the cat, you stay for the intelligence, and you leave with a smile.

## Verdict

A cozy Sokoban-style puzzle marrying feline charm and real depth, thanks to targeted explosions and the pipe mechanic: clever, polished and endearing, to savor in sessions.

**Strengths:**

- A Sokoban base enriched by two clever mechanics
- The controlled explosions that turn the puzzle into planning
- The pipe network that adds a clever spatial dimension
- A feline charm that's never a cover for emptiness

**Weaknesses:**

- A niche puzzle, to consume in sessions rather than in one go
- A lifespan hinging on the generosity of its levels
- Little replayability once the puzzles are solved
