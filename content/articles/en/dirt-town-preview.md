Dirt Town's promise fits in one line from its Steam page: you have not felt dirt like this in a game before.

![An excavator loading a dump truck in a quarry](/images/dirttown/screenshot-3.webp)

You drive heavy machinery and move dirt. Fell trees, move rocks, haul gravel, dig ditches to open up a road. The whole map is one simulation: you can plough, dig and pile anywhere, materials are layered (sand behaves nothing like clay), moisture affects grip, and rain creates creeks and puddles. Everything you do stays in your save.

The launch map focuses on extraction: find your way through the wilderness, break a road for the hauler, locate the deposits and ship them out. Side missions spice things up: build a dirt bike track and race it, jump a truck off a huge cliff, drain a swamp. You can auto-level slopes with stakes, tow with chains in multiplayer, and get out on foot to climb.

![A bulldozer spreading gravel beside a creek](/images/dirttown/screenshot-4.webp)

Skyturns plans a major snow update shortly after its Early Access launch on 4 December. The studio states that some 3D models are generated. And it warns that you need a decent computer to hold 60 frames per second.

Your finest ditch deserves a photo on the [InsertCoins Discord](https://discord.gg/473FE3dWvw). No judgement.
