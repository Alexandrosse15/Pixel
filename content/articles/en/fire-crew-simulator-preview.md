Fire Crew Simulator was announced for this week. The Steam page now says 10 December. All the more reason to look at what Dinomore Games has changed in recent months, because it is quite remarkable.

In September, the team posted a gameplay clip on Reddit and got well over a hundred comments. "Many were blunt, and most of them were right," they wrote. The lake was a flat cyan sheet; it now has waves and ripples under the rotor wash. The helicopter "read like an RC toy"; it was enlarged and its sound rebuilt from layered recordings. A second clip drew almost 90 comments from firefighters, pilots and players, and one of the first simply said "WAY more smoke". The smoke column is now lit by the fire itself, orange at the base, fading into the dark at the top.

![A red helicopter with its bucket over a burning forest](/images/firecrewsimulator/screenshot-1.webp)

The game itself is built for a team of four. Pilots fly with a bucket slung under the helicopter, someone drives the fire truck along the road, and together you stop the spread before the whole ridge goes up in smoke. No refill menu: you dip the bucket in a lake, let it sink, lift it out full and feel the weight. It swings on its cable with every turn.

![The fire truck on a mountain road, at the edge of the fire](/images/firecrewsimulator/screenshot-3.webp)

Two design ideas really appeal to me. Each player sets their own flight difficulty, so a beginner and a veteran share the same sky. And the more of you there are, the stronger the fire: with three or four, extra hotspots break out. Three helicopters are planned, the FC-1 Halcyon, the FC-2 Halcyon Twin and the FC-3 Atlas, a heavy-lift helicopter to unlock. A solo campaign is also available, from the lookout tower.

The forest is now Norway spruce and European beech, burnt-out trees become bare, bent, blackened trunks, and the game supports DLSS 4.5.

The bucket pilots of the [InsertCoins Discord](https://discord.gg/473FE3dWvw) are already looking for a truck driver. Apply within.
