There is now a whole genre of games that do not want your attention, just a corner of your screen. We covered [Drawquarium](/articles/drawquarium-test) here, the aquarium that lives on your desktop. Vibe Train does the same with a train.

![A regional map and the train crossing snowy mountains at the bottom of the screen](/images/vibetrain/screenshot-4.webp)

You build your train, pick a destination, arrange your wagons, and the convoy crosses a side-scrolling pixel art world on a strip at the bottom of your desktop while you work or study. Three compact display modes let you shrink the space it takes up.

It is not just an animated backdrop. You trade between regions, take on transport orders, carry passengers and sell tickets. Farming wagons turn the train into a rolling vegetable garden: you sow, and harvest during the trip. A tech tree unlocks locomotives and wagons, and a journal records the regions you discover.

![The locomotive and wagon unlock tree](/images/vibetrain/screenshot-2.webp)

IGNICORE says it spent recent weeks "staring at graphs and tweaking the curves" to rebalance the demo's economy, which players found out of balance. The game has passed Steam's review and will launch as planned on 27 November. The demo will be removed at launch.

Your favourite route deserves a share on the [InsertCoins Discord](https://discord.gg/473FE3dWvw), between two meetings.
