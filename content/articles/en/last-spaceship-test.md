There is something both melancholic and soothing about wandering an empty ship, relighting its dead lamps one by one and understanding, room after room, what played out there. Last Spaceship makes this spatial solitude its raw material, turning the restoration of a wreck into a small contemplative ritual punctuated by puzzles. It is slow, it is gentle, and it is far more gripping than its apparent calm suggests.

![Last Spaceship, exploring an abandoned ship](/images/lastspaceship/screenshot-1.webp)

## The context

Last Spaceship is developed by Normal Games and co-published with XAI LABS, available on PC via Steam. It is a cozy, atmospheric sci-fi puzzle adventure: you explore and restore an abandoned starship, solve interconnected puzzles, unlock new areas and unearth humanity's final legacy. Tagged adventure, casual and indie, the game owns a calm, meditative register, the opposite of action, where atmosphere counts as much as the cerebral challenge.

## The gameplay

The heart of the game is the explore, understand, repair, advance loop. The ship is not a mere backdrop to cross: it is a system to bring back to life, where each solved puzzle relights a function, opens a door, reveals one more fragment of the story. What makes Last Spaceship superior to run-of-the-mill puzzlers is precisely the word interconnected: the puzzles are not isolated boxes you tick off one by one, they talk to each other, and restoring a circuit in one section can unlock a mechanism at the other end of the ship. This global-system logic delivers the rewarding feeling of reviving an organism, not ticking off a list of brain-teasers.

Exploration rewards curiosity and sustains the mystery. Unlocking a new zone is both a mechanical progress and a narrative advance, because each reclaimed room tells a piece of the silent drama that emptied the ship. The game distils its story without a word too many, through the environment, the clues and the atmosphere, and this environmental storytelling lands: you push on as much to solve the next puzzle as to understand what truly happened aboard. This dual motivation, cerebral and narrative, is the real engine that keeps you going.

![Last Spaceship, solving interconnected puzzles](/images/lastspaceship/screenshot-2.webp)

The atmosphere is the game's great success, and it is not decorative. The inhabited silence of the ship, the light slowly returning, the bittersweet feeling of restoring the remnants of a vanished civilisation, all conspire toward a coherent, immersive contemplative experience. Last Spaceship understands that cozy is not a synonym for empty: it wraps its puzzle gameplay in an atmosphere that gives weight to every action, turning a simple repair mechanic into a meditation on endings and memory.

The game's limits stem from its scale and its stance. It is a short, calm experience, without punishing difficulty or tension, which will delight fans of the genre but leave cold those looking for a stiff challenge or a long adventure. Some puzzles, once the interconnection logic is grasped, resolve without much resistance, and you would sometimes have liked one extra notch of complexity to prolong the pleasure of deduction. These are reservations coherent with the game's calm ambition, not betrayals, but they bound the experience to a specific audience.

## The tech

Technically, Last Spaceship cares above all for its atmosphere, and that is the right call for a game built on immersion. The sci-fi art direction, at once cold and melancholic, serves the subject perfectly, the lighting paces the progression aptly, and the discreet sound design thickens the ship's inhabited silence. The whole composes a coherent atmosphere that carries the experience from end to end.

The technical reservations are minor. The legibility of some interconnected puzzles would sometimes benefit from better signalling the cause-and-effect links between distant sections, to avoid the blind back-and-forth that breaks the contemplative rhythm. But the game runs cleanly and never betrays its atmosphere through a glaring technical flaw, which is the essential thing for an adventure so centred on feeling.

![Last Spaceship, a restored zone and humanity's final legacy](/images/lastspaceship/screenshot-3.webp)

## The verdict

Last Spaceship wins its bet as a cozy puzzle adventure by refusing the easy way of contemplative emptiness. Its interconnected puzzles, reviving the ship like an organism rather than a checklist of brain-teasers, deliver genuine system satisfaction, and its environmental storytelling, distilled without a word too many, turns the restoration of a wreck into a gripping meditation on the end of humanity. The immersive, coherent atmosphere is the backbone of an experience worth the journey.

Its limits, its short length and gentle difficulty, flow directly from its contemplative identity and are only flaws for those who expected a stiff challenge. For fans of atmospheric adventures and clever puzzles to savour without pressure, Last Spaceship offers a short but memorable journey, the kind you do not entirely forget once the ship's lights are finally back on.

**A contemplative puzzle adventure that turns spatial solitude into a riddle as beautiful as it is melancholic.**

Score: 7/10
