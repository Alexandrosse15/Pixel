The Master grows impatient. He wants his imps, and you are going to fetch them, deep in a Purgatory that changes shape with every descent: hallways flooded with toxic waste, dark catacombs, haunted manors.

![A small hooded character in front of a toxic green portal](/images/snackrifice/screenshot-1.webp)

Snackrifice, from Karnage Studios, is a roguelike for up to four players in co-op, with proximity voice chat, which means you hear your friend scream when they get caught at the end of a corridor, and not before. Dungeons are procedural, traps and creatures plentiful, and each venture is more dangerous than the last.

The cleverest idea is the curses. More than 50 strange effects weigh on each expedition, but you pick which one to suffer. A way to turn difficulty into a decision. Between descents, Grimsly, the grumpy shopkeeper, sells relics for the Undercoins collected in Purgatory.

![A character facing the Master, huge and red, in a manor hall](/images/snackrifice/screenshot-3.webp)

The demo has been heavily reworked since the summer (bleeding, burning and poison, a new enemy, special rooms, expanded customisation), and the developer noted that the list of fixed bugs "would dwarf the entire Lord of the Rings trilogy". Out on 25 November, for 6.99 dollars.

Not to be confused with another Snackrifice, a free Spanish game about a devourer of Norse gods who wants to make a holy sandwich, due in December. Yes, two games about sacrifice and food share the same name this month.

Assemble your imp-hunting crew on the [InsertCoins Discord](https://discord.gg/473FE3dWvw). The Master does not wait.
