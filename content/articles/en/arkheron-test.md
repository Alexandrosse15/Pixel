Arkheron is the first game from Bonfire Studios, founded by Rob Pardo, Blizzard's former chief creative officer (World of Warcraft, StarCraft). You can feel it in how polished the combat is. Which makes what happened on Early Access launch day all the more frustrating: 42% positive out of the first 33 reviews, and many of the negative ones saying the same thing. The game is good, but there are not enough people to start a match.

![An arena fight around a circle of light](/images/arkheron/screenshot-4.webp)

## The Tower

The main mode, Ascension, sends teams of three into a Tower caught in a cycle of destruction and renewal. You fight monsters, collect items, battle rival teams for control of beacons, and climb floor by floor as others are eliminated. At the top, only two teams remain for the final showdown. It is a battle royale, but vertical and compact. The second mode, Spires, is a three-versus-three arena where you face opponent after opponent.

Everything rests on items. What you find gives you your abilities, and there are thousands of combinations. You do not pick a character with a fixed kit: you build your loadout during the match, Diablo-style, and it changes every game.

The free-aim isometric combat is the game's strong point. Cover, sightlines, dodges, enemy footsteps: there is real depth, and the best moments are fights turned around by an item combination the opponent did not see coming. Several players describe it as one of the best isometric combat systems they have played, and I can see why.

![A character striking in a room with crystal walls](/images/arkheron/screenshot-2.webp)

## The launch

The problem is the audience. Around 400 players online on launch day is very few for a 24.99 euro multiplayer game that needs to fill entire Towers. Players report endless queues, matches padded with bots, and others were put off by a sluggish feel and a locked camera.

Bonfire has ideas to fix this. The Friend Pass lets you invite a friend who does not own the game. And from 30 November to 6 December, partner servers (Discord communities and creators) let people play for free. The studio itself acknowledges the game is still far from the competitive experience it wants for 1.0, and lists fight readability as a priority. The game is also out on PS5 and Xbox Series.

![A group of heroes in a golden hall of the Tower](/images/arkheron/screenshot-3.webp)

There is an excellent game in the making here. But a PvP game without players is not a game, and the free week will tell whether Arkheron finds its audience.
