Business Simulator 2026 entered Early Access in November 2025 and leaves it on 25 November. The premise has not changed: you start from nothing, rent a first shop (stationery, pet store, gifts...), stock it, hire, automate, and open another. A convenience store sim multiplied by the number of stores.

![A busy shop, with checkouts and customers](/images/businesssimulator2026/screenshot-3.webp)

Version 1.0 brings two substantial additions. First, a headquarters: at company level 30, you can buy the twin towers downtown for a million dollars. Your office carries your logo, and from its computer you manage all your shops remotely, without crossing town. All your shops gain 25% XP.

Second, online sales. With a licence, orders come in during opening hours. You pack boxes, load up to four into your pickup and deliver by following the minimap. Orders expire after a day.

![The city map and shops for rent](/images/businesssimulator2026/screenshot-2.webp)

On top of that: reworked lighting, a redesigned interface and a long list of fixes, including customers no longer getting stuck on their way. Early Access earned 71% positive reviews out of 185, a decent reception and no more. HOGO Games also states that some Steam store visuals and in-game product images were generated.
